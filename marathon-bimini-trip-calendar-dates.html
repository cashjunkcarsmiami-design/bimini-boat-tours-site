Checking Calendar Dates Across a Marathon and Bimini Trip

Bimini Boat Tours · October 23, 2026

When a trip includes time in Marathon and a separate visit to Bimini, a message such as ‘next Friday’ can become unclear as plans change. Use the complete calendar date when discussing the boat outing, accommodation and travel arrangements.

Write dates beside each separate booking

Record the arrival date, proposed outing date and onward travel date in distinct lines. Ask each provider to confirm the local time used in its instructions rather than converting times from memory. Keep tentative requests visibly marked as tentative. A place on your calendar is a planning note until the provider confirms the arrangement.

Recheck after moving one part of the trip

A changed hotel night does not automatically change the boat reservation. Contact the relevant providers and review their terms before assuming an adjustment is possible. This page is for planning a Bimini outing during a wider trip; it does not establish a departure from Marathon or an included transfer. Send the group the corrected dates and clearly identify which earlier plan they replace.
