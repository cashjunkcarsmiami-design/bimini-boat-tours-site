Planning a Surprise During a Bimini Outing from Miami

Bimini Boat Tours · October 23, 2026

A surprise for a companion needs coordination before the outing, especially when your group is traveling from Miami. Describe the idea to the tour team before promising decorations, music or a special activity. The operator must confirm what the boat and itinerary can accommodate.

Separate the occasion from the request

Explain the occasion and list the specific items you want to bring or arrange. Ask about permitted decorations, cleanup and any restrictions. Do not send private personal details about the recipient when a brief description will do. Keep one organizer responsible for communicating changes so a surprise does not become several conflicting plans.

Keep the surprise out of essential instructions

Everyone still needs the real meeting location, timing and packing instructions. Confirm travel to Bimini separately and avoid presenting this page as a Miami departure booking. If the special request cannot be accommodated, choose an alternative with the operator before confirming. Do not withhold information the crew needs to assess the outing, and do not treat an informal idea as an included service.
