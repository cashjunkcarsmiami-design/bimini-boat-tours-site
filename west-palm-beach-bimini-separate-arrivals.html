BIMINI TRIP PLANNING

Coordinating Separate Arrivals for a Bimini Group from West Palm Beach

October 19, 2026

A group organized from West Palm Beach may include travelers arriving in Bimini on different days or through separate providers. Before choosing the outing date, collect each person’s confirmed plans. The earliest arrival in the group does not establish when everyone will be ready to board.

Build a simple arrival list

Record each guest’s expected arrival, where they will be staying and how they plan to reach the meeting point. Mark unconfirmed arrangements clearly. Share only the relevant timing and group details with the tour team; there is no need to circulate everyone’s full travel documents in a group chat.

Choose one coordinator and a backup

Have one person relay changes to the tour team, with another contact available if needed. Ask how late arrivals affect the booking and whether a changed guest count needs approval. Do not assume the boat can make extra pickups or wait indefinitely because guests have separate travel plans.

Confirm the final meeting instructions

After the team confirms the location and time, distribute one final message to all guests. Include the correct contact and distinguish the tour meeting point from a hotel lobby or transportation terminal. A local planning page for West Palm Beach does not confirm a departure there; each travel and transfer arrangement needs its own confirmation.
