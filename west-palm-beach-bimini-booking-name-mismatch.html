When a Bimini Tour Booking Uses a Different Contact Name

Bimini Boat Tours · October 23, 2026

A group traveling from West Palm Beach may use one name for the inquiry and another for the accommodation or travel reservation. Keep those arrangements distinct so messages reach the person actually coordinating the boat outing.

Identify the organizer consistently

Ask which name or booking reference the tour team uses to find your inquiry. Give the same reference when following up, and explain if another traveler will handle questions. Do not assume a hotel reservation number identifies a separate boat booking. Avoid sharing identity documents in a group message just to establish who is organizing the trip.

Resolve changes with each provider

If the lead contact changes, ask the operator to confirm the updated contact details and who will receive the final instructions. Travelers should still know which provider handles transport to Bimini and which handles the outing. Review the actual meeting arrangements before traveling; an inquiry under your name is not proof of a confirmed booking or a direct departure from West Palm Beach.
