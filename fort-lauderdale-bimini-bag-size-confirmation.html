Bag Size Questions for Bimini Travelers from Fort Lauderdale

Bimini Boat Tours · October 24, 2026

Travelers planning a Bimini outing from Fort Lauderdale may arrive with bags sized for a longer vacation. Before bringing all of them to the boat, ask what baggage can be accommodated on the specific outing being considered. Space and storage should be confirmed rather than inferred from a promotional photograph.

Describe the actual items

Tell the operator how many bags your group expects to carry and whether any item is unusually large or rigid. Ask about storage and what should remain elsewhere. Do not assume the operator provides luggage holding before or after the outing. Arrange any separate storage with the relevant provider and confirm access hours directly.

Plan around separate travel arrangements

Your transportation to Bimini may have its own baggage conditions, which need to be checked with that provider. Those conditions do not establish what can go on a local boat outing. Once the group agrees on a practical packing plan, share it with everyone so extra bags do not appear unexpectedly. Confirm the meeting point and final outing details independently; this planning guide does not establish a departure from Fort Lauderdale.
