Miami to Bimini Departure-Day Preparation

Reduce last-minute confusion by confirming the meeting plan, group details and flexible itinerary in advance.

Confirm the actual meeting point

Do not assume that a Miami-area inquiry means a particular marina. Ask for the exact departure location, arrival time, parking instructions and the name or phone number of the trip contact. Plan extra time for local traffic and marina access.

Recheck every traveler’s information

Confirm names and required passenger details by the operator’s deadline. International travel-document and entry requirements depend on current rules and individual circumstances, so check official government sources and the operator rather than relying on an old checklist.

Pack for a boat, not a hotel

Use compact, water-resistant bags. Ask what food, water, towels, snorkeling equipment and safety gear are included. Bring necessary medication, sun protection and a dry change of clothes. Guests concerned about motion sickness should seek qualified medical guidance in advance.

Expect a condition-dependent plan

The captain may adjust, delay or cancel a trip because of marine conditions. Confirm how updates and rescheduling terms are communicated. Keep snorkeling, sandbar and sightseeing preferences flexible until the day’s safe route is known.
