Two Organizers, One Bimini Outing: Planning from Marathon

Bimini Boat Tours · October 24, 2026

When two people organize a Bimini outing alongside a Marathon vacation, each may receive different information as the plan develops. Choose how you will keep the group updated before requesting changes. Two active conversations should not accidentally become two different versions of the same outing.

Assign one confirmation contact

Decide who will send the final date, group size and questions to the operator. The other organizer can collect preferences without presenting them as confirmed arrangements. If the main contact changes, tell the operator and ask which conversation should be used going forward. Keep personal travel information limited to what is needed for coordination.

Review one current summary

Before anyone makes dependent plans, compare the latest written response with the group's expectations. Mark older proposals as superseded and ask about anything that conflicts. Do not assume that an outing begins in Marathon or includes transport to Bimini; confirm travel separately. A shared summary should identify confirmed details and open questions without turning a preferred stop, schedule or amenity into a promise the operator has not made.
