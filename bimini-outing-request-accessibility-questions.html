How to Describe Access Needs When Asking About a Bimini Outing

Bimini Boat Tours · October 25, 2026

If a guest has practical access needs, discuss them with Bimini Boat Tours before deciding on an outing. A photograph of a boat does not show every boarding condition or establish suitability for a particular person. Focus on the assistance or physical arrangement the guest needs, without sharing unnecessary private information.

Ask about the specific arrangement

Explain the relevant practical questions, such as steps, boarding assistance or space for an item the guest uses. Ask the operator what can be confirmed for the actual boat and proposed meeting point. Do not assume that an accommodation available on a previous trip is available on this one. If a detail remains uncertain, keep it identified as an open question.

Let the guest review the response

Share the operator's description with the person concerned so they can consider whether the arrangement meets their needs. Do not promise assistance on the crew's behalf. Confirm any agreed arrangements before booking and ask what happens if the boat or meeting location changes. Travel to Bimini is separate unless explicitly included, so access questions for that journey should also be addressed with its own provider.
