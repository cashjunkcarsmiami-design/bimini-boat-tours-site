Parking and Rental-Car Handoffs When Adding Bimini to a Marathon Vacation

Bimini Boat Tours · October 29, 2026

Combining a Marathon stay with a separate Bimini outing can involve a vehicle you must park or return before continuing your journey. Settle those arrangements around the confirmed travel plan, without assuming a tour includes parking or a transfer.

Confirm where each part begins

Write down the address and date for your rental return or parking reservation, your separately booked travel departure and your Bimini tour meeting point. Ask the responsible provider to clarify any unclear location. A planning inquiry from Marathon does not establish a direct boat departure from the Florida Keys.

Ask the parking or rental provider

Confirm access hours, reservation requirements, return instructions and who to contact if plans change. Ask whether the parking arrangement permits your expected length of stay and how you retrieve the vehicle. For a rental, use the rental company’s written instructions for the actual return location rather than assuming any nearby branch can receive it.

Account for people and bags after the handoff

Work out how everyone reaches the next confirmed meeting point once the vehicle is parked or returned. Check the permitted luggage with each provider and arrange storage separately if needed. Do not leave the organizer’s only copy of meeting instructions inside the car. Keep the booking references accessible to the person traveling with the group.

Revisit the handoff if dates change

A change to the Bimini outing does not automatically extend parking, lodging or a rental reservation. Contact each provider to check availability and applicable terms before accepting a revised schedule. Share the updated plan with the group, clearly separating confirmed arrangements from requests. Ask Bimini Boat Tours about the outing itself and confirm transportation and vehicle arrangements with their respective providers.
