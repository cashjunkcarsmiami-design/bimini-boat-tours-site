Bringing Personal Snorkeling Gear on a Bimini Outing

Bimini Boat Tours · October 23, 2026

Travelers from Fort Lauderdale sometimes prefer their own snorkeling equipment. Before packing it for a Bimini outing, ask the operator what is allowed and what the proposed trip actually includes. Owning equipment does not guarantee that a snorkeling stop is part of the confirmed itinerary.

Describe the equipment before departure

List masks, fins and any bulky bags you intend to carry. Ask about storage and whether the crew needs to review the equipment before use. Do not assume another operator's packing advice applies to this boat. Follow the tour team's instructions and avoid adding equipment that has not been discussed simply because there is room in your travel suitcase.

Confirm the activity separately

Ask what arrangements apply to guests who prefer sightseeing instead. Share preferences before accepting a booking and let the operator assess conditions and the itinerary. Plan travel to Bimini and baggage arrangements with the appropriate providers; this page does not establish a direct Fort Lauderdale departure. If your equipment plan changes, update the team rather than arriving with unexpected bulky items.
