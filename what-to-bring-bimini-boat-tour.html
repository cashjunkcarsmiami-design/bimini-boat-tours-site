Bimini Boat Tour Packing List

  • Required passports, identification, immigration documents, and booking confirmations.
  • Reef-conscious sunscreen, sunglasses with a strap, hat, and light sun-protective clothing.
  • Swimsuit, towel, dry change of clothes, and a small waterproof bag.
  • Personal medicine and motion-sickness products approved by your medical professional.
  • Waterproof phone case and a charged battery pack.
  • Water and approved snacks after confirming what the boat provides.

Confirm Documents Before Travel

Bimini is in The Bahamas. Travel and entry requirements can change, so every guest should verify current government and carrier requirements before departure. Names on reservations and documents should match.

Dress for Sun, Wind, and Water

Quick-drying layers are useful because conditions can feel sunny, windy, or cool during travel. Avoid bulky luggage and items that can blow away. Ask before bringing large coolers, fishing gear, or special equipment.

Share Group Needs in Advance

Tell the crew about children, guests who are not strong swimmers, mobility considerations, allergies, and the stops your group prefers. Weather and water conditions may require route changes.
