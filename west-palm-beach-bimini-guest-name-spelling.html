Correcting a Guest Name in a Bimini Booking Conversation

Bimini Boat Tours · October 26, 2026

A misspelled name can make it harder to match messages when several people organize a Bimini visit from West Palm Beach. Correct it early and explain whether the change concerns the booking contact or a different guest. Those are not necessarily the same person.

Use the existing conversation

Reply with the name as it should appear and identify the planned date so the operator can locate the inquiry. Avoid opening several new requests for a simple correction. Ask what guest information is actually required and which channel should be used before sending documents or personal details.

Distinguish a correction from a substitution

Changing a spelling is different from replacing one guest with another or changing who manages the booking. Explain which situation applies and request any relevant next steps. Keep the corrected confirmation available to the organizer. This helps the group recognize its own arrangements without assuming that every provider involved in the wider trip shares the same records.
