Protecting Small Personal Items During a Bimini Outing

Bimini Boat Tours · October 26, 2026

For travelers planning a Bimini visit from Fort Lauderdale, a phone, wallet and spare clothing need a practical storage plan. Do not assume that a boat outing includes dry storage for every guest or that a bag described as water resistant can be submerged.

Ask what storage is available

Discuss the size and number of bags with the operator before packing. Ask whether there is a suitable place for small personal items and what conditions belongings may encounter. Keep unnecessary valuables out of the outing plan. Confirm arrangements for your particular booking rather than relying on a storage compartment visible in an older photo.

Make access simple for your group

Organize items so you can find what you need without emptying several bags. Keep important travel documents protected and follow the operator's guidance about where belongings may be placed. Your trip from Fort Lauderdale to Bimini is a separate planning step unless explicitly included; allow for how you will manage luggage before and after the outing.
