Planning a Screen-Free Bimini Outing During a Marathon Vacation

Bimini Boat Tours · October 26, 2026

Some travelers adding Bimini to a Marathon vacation want time away from phones and constant photos. That preference is worth discussing with the group before the outing. It can shape how you enjoy your time without requiring the operator to promise a particular route or activity.

Agree on a simple group preference

Decide whether guests would like occasional photos, a shared camera or mostly uninterrupted time together. Ask the operator about any practical communication needs during the outing. A preference for putting phones away should not prevent the organizer from receiving important booking or meeting updates before boarding.

Keep essential arrangements accessible

Save the confirmed meeting details and know who is responsible for checking messages while the group travels. Avoid relying on one person's memory for the time and location. Travel between your Marathon stay and Bimini remains separate unless specifically confirmed. Once the practical details are settled, the group can choose how much attention it wants to give screens.
