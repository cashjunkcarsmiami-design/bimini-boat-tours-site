What to Reconfirm If Your Proposed Bimini Tour Boat Changes

Bimini Boat Tours · October 29, 2026

A replacement boat can affect the details your group used to choose a private Bimini outing. Before accepting an updated plan, compare the actual arrangement with the features and terms previously discussed.

Start with the written change

Ask which boat is now proposed, why the plan is changing and whether your date, meeting point or trip length changes too. Keep the latest message with your booking reference. A picture of another boat is useful context, but ask the operator to identify the features of the boat actually being offered.

Recheck the needs that shaped your decision

Review the confirmed guest count, seating, shade, restroom access and space for permitted bags. If anyone has a practical boarding need, describe that need again and ask whether the replacement arrangement can accommodate it. Avoid assuming that two boats with a similar appearance have the same layout or equipment.

Ask how activities and price are affected

Discuss the stops your group requested, the activities the operator can currently offer and anything that remains dependent on conditions. Ask whether inclusions, exclusions, price, payment timing or cancellation and change terms differ. Do not treat a boat substitution as automatic confirmation of the same itinerary or of an upgrade.

Share one current decision with the group

Have the booking contact confirm whether the group accepts the revised arrangement and request an updated written summary. Mark older messages as superseded so guests do not follow an outdated meeting instruction. If a feature essential to your group is unresolved, ask for clarification before accepting. Travel to Bimini and an outing in Bimini remain separate arrangements unless expressly confirmed together.
