West Palm Beach Bimini Group Meeting Checklist

Travelers coming from different homes or hotels need one shared plan. Organize contact information, timing and instructions before the proposed trip rather than relying on separate message threads.

Choose the Group Coordinator

One person should communicate with the operator and share the confirmed details. The coordinator should know the final group size and remain available for updates.

Do Not Guess the Meeting Point

Wait for the operator to provide the exact location and arrival instructions. Confirm parking, access and the time travelers should be ready.

Plan the Trip to the Meeting Location

Group members should account for their own starting points, traffic, parking and any overnight arrangements. Build a reasonable buffer without inventing a departure time that has not been confirmed.

Confirm Documents and Personal Needs

Ask which documents apply to the proposed itinerary and verify official requirements. Share information about children, mobility needs, first-time boaters or other comfort considerations during planning.

Create an Update Plan

Decide how the coordinator will reach everyone if instructions change. Make sure travelers check messages before leaving and understand the operator’s cancellation and weather process.

Carry the Confirmed Details

Keep the operator contact, meeting instructions, group list, inclusions and payment confirmation together. Do not rely on an old screenshot if the plan has changed.
