Essential Bimini Boat Tour Packing List

Pack lightly, but bring the items needed for a full day around sun and water. Confirm the departure point, trip length, included equipment and travel-document requirements before leaving. Requirements can vary based on where your trip begins and your itinerary.

  • Government-issued identification and any required travel documents.
  • Reef-conscious sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and sun-protective clothing.
  • Swimwear, a towel and a dry change of clothes.
  • Non-slip boat shoes or secure water footwear.
  • Necessary medication in its original packaging.
  • A reusable water bottle and any approved snacks.
  • A small waterproof bag for phones, wallets and personal items.
  • A waterproof phone case or camera strap for photos.

Ask What Is Included

Before buying or packing extra equipment, ask what the boat provides. Depending on the trip, guests may need to confirm snorkeling gear, life jackets for children, drinking water, coolers, food rules and storage space. Correct sizing matters for masks, fins and safety equipment.

Prepare for Weather and Motion

Wind, waves, tide and visibility can change a route or planned stop. Bring a light water-resistant layer and secure loose belongings. Anyone concerned about motion sickness should speak with a medical professional before the trip and follow product directions for any approved remedy.

Pack for the Bimini Experience You Want

Different activities call for slightly different preparation. Snorkeling guests should ask about equipment and swimming ability. Sandbar groups may want extra sun protection and a waterproof camera. Sapona visitors should follow the captain's instructions around the site. Wildlife sightings, including dolphins, are never guaranteed and should always be approached responsibly.

Before You Leave

  1. Confirm your date, meeting place and arrival time.
  2. Verify travel documents and any entry requirements that apply.
  3. Share the group size, ages and swimming comfort level.
  4. Ask what food, drinks and equipment are included or permitted.
  5. Check the operator's weather update and cancellation terms.
  6. Tell the crew about accessibility or safety needs in advance.

Do not bring unnecessary valuables. Keep documents and electronics protected from spray, and follow all captain and crew instructions throughout the day.

Bimini Boat Tour Packing FAQ

What should I bring on a Bimini boat tour?

Bring sun protection, swimwear, a towel, secure footwear, water, required documents, necessary medication and a waterproof bag. Confirm what the operator supplies.

Should I bring snorkeling equipment?

Ask whether equipment is included and what sizes are available. A properly fitted mask can improve comfort when personal equipment is permitted.

Can weather change the route?

Yes. Marine conditions can affect timing, stops and departure. Plans should remain flexible, and the captain's safety decision must be followed.
