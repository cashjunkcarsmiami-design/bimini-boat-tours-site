Planning a Bimini Boat Trip for a Multi-Generational Family

A family group may include experienced swimmers, young travelers and relatives who prefer to stay aboard. The itinerary should account for everyone.

Describe the complete group

Share ages, swimming confidence, mobility considerations and boating experience. Ask whether the specific vessel and activities are appropriate for every traveler.

Choose optional activities

Confirm whether snorkeling and swimming are optional and what non-swimmers can do during water stops. Prioritize one or two experiences instead of rushing through a long list.

Ask about practical comfort

Discuss shade, seating, restroom availability, food rules, medication storage and permitted bags. Guests with medical concerns should seek qualified advice before offshore travel.
