Private Bimini Tour Planning Tips

Weather matters in Bimini. Wind, tide, and visibility can change which route is best for the day. A strong private boat tour plan should stay flexible, especially when guests want snorkeling, sandbar time, Sapona views, or dolphin watching possibilities in one trip. The goal is to create a good day on the water without forcing the boat into a route that does not match conditions.

Tell the operator your preferred activities, date, group size and any comfort or boarding needs. Ask which requests can fit the proposed boat, duration and conditions. Keep the final route, meeting point and complete purchase terms together before confirming.
