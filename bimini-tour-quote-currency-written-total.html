Currency and Total-Price Questions for a Bimini Boat Quote

Bimini Boat Tours · October 24, 2026

A number in a message is not a complete price description. When requesting an outing with Bimini Boat Tours, ask which currency the quote uses, what the quoted amount covers, and whether it applies to your whole group or another stated basis. Do this before comparing it with a different proposal.

Request an itemized explanation

Ask the operator to distinguish the outing price from any separately charged items and to state which costs remain unknown. If a deposit is proposed, ask how it relates to the total and which payment terms apply. Do not assume that a symbol, an old screenshot, or another traveler's quote describes the terms available for your date.

Keep the confirmed version together

Save the written quote with the requested date, group size and agreed scope. If any of those details change, request an updated total before deciding. Travel to Bimini and the local outing should be treated as separate arrangements unless their inclusion is explicitly confirmed. A clear price question does not commit you to a booking; it helps establish what you would actually be agreeing to.
