What to Pack for a Bimini Boat Tour

A useful packing list begins with the confirmed departure plan, trip length, activities and operator instructions. Ask what is included before buying or carrying extra equipment.

Confirm documents before packing

International travel requirements can vary by citizenship, age, departure method and current government rules. Ask the operator which documents guests must bring and verify requirements with the appropriate U.S. and Bahamian authorities. Do not rely on an old social-media post or another traveler's experience.

Keep documents protected from water but accessible when required. Confirm whether children need additional documentation and whether any guest has a name difference between reservations and identification.

Sun and heat protection

Consider reef-conscious sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses with a secure strap and lightweight clothing that covers exposed skin. Apply sun protection according to the product instructions and remember that glare from clear water can increase exposure.

Ask what drinking water and shade are available. Travelers should discuss personal hydration or heat concerns with a qualified medical professional when appropriate.

Clothing and footwear

Pack a swimsuit, dry change of clothes and a light layer for wind or cooler conditions. Ask what footwear is appropriate for the boat and planned stops. Avoid loose items that may blow away or create a tripping hazard.

A small waterproof or water-resistant bag can separate dry items from wet clothing. Label essential belongings for group trips so they are easier to identify.

Medication and personal needs

Bring required medication in its original labeled container and keep it with the guest who needs it. Ask a medical professional about motion sickness or travel concerns rather than relying on general advice. Tell the operator about mobility, allergy or other important needs early enough to discuss whether the requested trip is appropriate.

Phones, cameras and valuables

Use a protective case or dry bag for electronics. Bring only valuables you can manage securely. Confirm access to charging before assuming it will be available, and download important contact details before departure in case connectivity changes.

Activity-specific questions

For a Bimini snorkeling tour, ask whether masks, snorkels, fins and flotation equipment are provided and what sizes are available. For a sandbar stop, ask about footwear, depth and changing conditions. For a Sapona trip, confirm the planned viewing or activity expectations.

Group packing organization

Create one shared checklist instead of asking every traveler to bring the same group item. Assign responsibility for documents, child essentials, celebration items and backup contact information. Avoid bringing prohibited, fragile or excessive items without approval.

Packing FAQ

Should I bring snorkeling equipment?

Ask what the specific trip includes before bringing personal gear.

Can I bring food and drinks?

Confirm the operator's rules, storage limits and what is already included.

What should stay behind?

Avoid unnecessary valuables and any item the operator or authorities prohibit. Ask before bringing large coolers, decorations or specialized equipment.
