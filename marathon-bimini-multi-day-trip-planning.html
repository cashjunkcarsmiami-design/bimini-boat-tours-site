BIMINI TRIP PLANNING

Combining a Marathon Stay with a Separate Bimini Visit

October 19, 2026

A stay in Marathon and an outing in Bimini can be parts of the same vacation, but they should not be treated as one confirmed service. Start with the dates you expect to be in each place. Then identify what has been reserved and what is still only an idea before requesting a boat-tour date.

Map the separate reservations

List your accommodation, travel to Bimini and proposed tour as separate items. Ask each provider for its actual start location, timing and terms. The Marathon planning page helps travelers inquire about Bimini tours; it does not promise a direct crossing, local boat departure or transfer from the Florida Keys.

Avoid building the trip around an assumed route

Confirm how your group will reach Bimini before using that arrival time to choose an outing. Ask the tour team where you must meet and whether you need a separate local transfer. Verify current travel-document requirements with the relevant official authorities and transportation provider for your own circumstances.

Keep changes connected without combining policies

If one reservation changes, contact the other affected providers and explain the new dates. Ask about alternatives and any costs before accepting changes. One provider’s cancellation does not automatically cancel or refund the rest of the trip. Keep written confirmations together so the group can see which arrangements remain valid.
